**Meaning of Doom Scrolling:**

Doom Scrolling means, The compulsive act of continuously scrolling through and consuming negative, upsetting, or depressing news/social media content, even though it causes distress or anxiety.



**N.D.S (No Doom Scrolling):**

We know how doom scrolling consumes our time and it provides us nothing except mindlessness, leading to stress and reduced well-being and anxiety.

We don't even remember what was the first reel we saw or any other content we've watched, So N.D.S is a project which helps to Quit the Doom scrolling.

&#x20;The features are Listed below:

1. Selection of apps
2. Strict Timers, setting up a timer up to 1 hour a day (Maximum is 1 hour, only for Instagram) no opening after the completion of app's Time limit
3. No reset or removal option for the Limit after the completion
4. Online Mode, You can make groups and compete with your friends and family
5. Study Time table for Students with Notification
6. Scheduling tasks with calendar
7. After the completion of limit NO Excuses like "Ignore the limit for the day" or "Only use for 5, 10, 15 mins" none of this sh\*t will be present
8. Scheduled time limit, Means the app will be unlocked for specific time and the app will be automatically closed after the completion of time
(Like, only from 6pm to 7pm ).



**Benefits of this app:**

1. Saves a lot of time
2. Controlled Phone Addiction
3. Our Brain rewires to do the work which we were procrastinating
4. Helps us to be more Productive
5. For students more time to study
